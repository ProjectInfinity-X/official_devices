installing it, flashing Android 16/15 ROMs or older stock firmware can
permanently brick the device. Read the Android 17 installation guide.

- Update CarrierConfigOverlay to V1TDS35H.83-20-5-12.

- Build wlan_assistant from source.
- Switch Bluetooth audio to the AOSP implementation.
- Update the VT stack from Motorola nice A171VNH.25.
- Update the GNSS stack from ALPS 14 V10.69.
- Deduplicate dynamic sensor HAL SELinux rules.
- Clean up unused blobs.
