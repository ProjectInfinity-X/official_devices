Device: POCO F1

Flashing Instructions for InfinityX Recovery (Recommended Method):

**Before start flashing.....**
- Backup all your internal storage
- Download latest recovery.img from here
- Links-: https://sourceforge.net/projects/infinity-x/files/beryllium/17/recovery/recovery.img/download

**Clean flash**
1. Reboot to fastboot
2. ```fastboot flash recovery recovery.img```
3. Reboot to recovery
4. Select Factory reset and Format data/factory reset
5. Select Apply Update and Apply Update from ADB
6. Now connect your phone to PC and type ```adb sideload InfinityX.zip```
7. Reboot to system and enjoy :)

**Dirty flash** 
1. Reboot to recovery
2. Select Apply Update and Apply Update from ADB
3. Connect your phone to PC and flash rom with ```adb sideload infinityX.zip```
4. Reboot to system and enjoy :)

**OTA**:
1. Simply upgrade via ota updater (either if pushed or via local update import)


Flashing Instructions for OrangeFox Recovery (Alternate Method):

**Before start flashing.....**
- Backup all your internal storage
- Download OrangeFox Recovery from here
- Links-: https://github.com/ThRE-Team/sdm845_fox/releases/tag/25716499605

**Clean Flash**:
1. Download OrangeFox Recovery zip and flash it in current recovery either by sideload or via SD Card
2. Wipe all partitions except SD Card and OTG
3. Flash Rom zip
4. Format data (Just to be safe)
5. Reboot to system and enjoy :)

**Dirty Flash**:
1. Download Rom zip
2. Reboot to recovery
3. Flash Rom zip
4. Wipe Cache and Dalvik
5. Reboot to system and enjoy :)
