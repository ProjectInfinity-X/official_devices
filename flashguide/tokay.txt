Clean install

Grab the ROM zip from the gapps folder and boot.img, dtbo.img, vendor_kernel_boot.img and vendor_boot.img from IMGs here: https://sourceforge.net/projects/infinity-x/files/tokay/17/
The images in IMGs are always from the latest build.

1. Flash the October 2026 stock, CP3A.261005.005, with https://flash.android.com and boot it once. Pick that build yourself: the site offers the newest one, and newer stock doesn't work with this ROM yet. Tick Wipe device and Force flash all partitions, leave Lock bootloader unticked.
2. Reboot to the bootloader and flash the images:
   fastboot flash boot boot.img
   fastboot flash dtbo dtbo.img
   fastboot flash vendor_kernel_boot vendor_kernel_boot.img
   fastboot flash vendor_boot vendor_boot.img
   fastboot reboot recovery
3. In recovery: Factory reset -> Format data/factory reset.
4. Apply update -> Apply from ADB, then on your PC:
   adb sideload <ROM zip>
5. Reboot system now. The first boot takes a few minutes, that's normal.

Updating

Just use the updater in Settings, or sideload the new zip in recovery like in step 4, no formatting and no images needed. The zip brings its own bootloader and modem, so don't flash newer stock on top.

Coming from my unofficial builds? Do a clean install once, they were signed with different keys.

And please never lock the bootloader with a custom ROM on the phone, it won't boot anymore.
