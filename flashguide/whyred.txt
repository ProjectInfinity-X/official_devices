Device: Redmi Note 5 Pro (whyred)

Flashing Instructions:

* Clean Flash:
- Flash dynamic recovery
- First time only (coming from MIUI/stock or a ROM without /metadata):
  format logdump as ext4, it is used as /metadata
  (recovery terminal: mke2fs -t ext4 /dev/block/by-name/logdump)
- Wipe dalvik cache
- Do format data
- Reboot to recovery
- Do format data
- Reboot to recivery
- Flash rom
- Do cache and data f2fs
- Reboot system

* Dirty Flash:
- Download rom
- Reboot to rom
- Wipe dalvik cache
- Flash rom
- Reboot to system
