Device: Poco X8 Pro 5G (klee)
Recommended boot.img:https://sourceforge.net/projects/bezke/files/klee-fenrir/boot.img/download
Recommenden vendor_boot.img:https://sourceforge.net/projects/bezke/files/klee-fenrir/vendor_boot.img/download

Flashing Instructions:

* Clean Flash:

1. Reboot into the bootloader: adb reboot bootloader

2. Flash `vendor_boot.img`: fastboot flash vendor_boot /path/to/vendor_boot.img

3. Flash `boot.img`: fastboot flash boot /path/to/boot.img

4. Reboot the device: fastboot reboot

5. As soon as the device starts rebooting, immediately hold the **Volume Up** button to enter recovery.

6. In recovery, select: Factory Reset → Format Data

7. Then select: Apply Update → Apply from ADB

8. Sideload the ROM: adb sideload /path/to/Project_Infinity-X-4.0-klee-...-OFFICIAL.zip
   
* Dirty Flash:
1. Reboot into the recovery

2. Sideload the ROM: adb sideload /path/to/Project_Infinity-X-4.0-klee-...-OFFICIAL.zip

* OTA:
1. Simply upgrade via ota updater (either if pushed or via local update import)
