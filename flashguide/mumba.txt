Device: Motorola G57 Power (mumba)

Recovery: https://sourceforge.net/projects/infinity-x/files/mumba/17/recovery.img/download
Vendor boot: https://sourceforge.net/projects/infinity-x/files/mumba/17/vendor_boot.img/download
- The recovery and vendor_boot provided above are required, a custom recovery is not supported.

!!! WARNING !!!
===============
It is COMPULSORY to sideload copy-partitions-20220613-signed.zip before flashing the ROM.
Failing to do so can HARD-BRICK your device.
Download: https://mirrorbits.lineageos.org/tools/copy-partitions-20220613-signed.zip

Requirements:
=============
- Unlocked bootloader
- Full data backup and an IMEI backup
- At least 30% battery
- First boot may take 5-10 minutes, do not interrupt it


Clean Flash:
============
1. Reboot to bootloader (hold Power + Volume Down)

2. Flash the recovery and vendor_boot images:
   fastboot flash recovery recovery.img
   fastboot flash vendor_boot vendor_boot.img

3. Reboot to recovery:
   fastboot reboot recovery

4. Factory reset -> Format data / Factory reset

5. Apply update -> Apply from ADB, then sideload copy-partitions-20220613-signed.zip (MANDATORY):
   adb sideload copy-partitions-20220613-signed.zip

6. Apply update -> Apply from ADB, then sideload the ROM:
   adb sideload ROM.zip

7. If you want GApps and are flashing the vanilla build, reboot to recovery again and sideload the GApps package.
   (The GAPPS build already includes Google apps.)

8. Factory reset -> Format data / Factory reset

9. Reboot system now


Dirty Flash:
============
1. Reboot to recovery

2. Apply update -> Apply from ADB, then sideload the ROM:
   adb sideload ROM.zip

3. Vanilla build only: reflash your GApps package after every update

4. Reboot

(Not supported across major Android upgrades.)


OTA:
====
1. Simply update from Settings
2. Vanilla build only: reflash your GApps package after every update
